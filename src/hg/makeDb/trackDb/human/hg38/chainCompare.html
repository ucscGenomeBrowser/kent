Description

Tracks showing chain/liftOver comparisons for different alignment methods. Target assembly: GRCh38/hg38, Query assembly: hs1/T2T-CHM13.
      The liftOver chain from the T2T project
      The liftOver chain from the UCSC procedure 'Same Species' BLAT alignment
      The liftOver chain from the UCSC procedure lastz/chain/net alignment
      The liftOver chain from the miniMap2 alignment
Tracks shown for each type of alignment procedure:
  • the 'liftOver' chain
  • the 'all' chain before liftOver filtering
  • the 'Quick Lift' chain file from the same alignment procedure on the query hs1/CHM13
  • the 'raw' chain output of miniMap2 before applying the UCSC axtChain | chainBridge
  • the items from Gencode V50 genePred that failed the lift through the liftOver file. mouseOver shows the 'reason' for the failed lift.
  • Intersections of the liftOver files, a 'common' track showing what each of the 4 lift over chain files had in common, and a 'unique' track for each method showing what it has unique from the others.

Methods

The track data was obtained:
  • T2T liftOver - from files supplied by the CHM13 T2T project
  • BLAT/sameSpecies - result from UCSC procedure doSameSpeciesLiftOver.pl - uses: axtChain | chainBridge, linearGap=medium, minScore=1000, scoreScheme default HOXD70
  • lastz/chain/net - result from UCSC procedure doBlastzChainNet.pl - uses: axtChain | chainAntiRepeat, linearGap=medium, minScore=5000, scoreScheme=human_chimp.v2.q
  • miniMap2 - result from UCSC procedure doMiniMap2.pl - uses: axtChain | chainBridge | pafToPsl, arguments: -cx asm5 -N 5 -p 0.8 -t 8

  • Partially deleted in new - light red 128,0,0,
  • Boundary problem - blue 0,0,255,
  • Can't find thickStart/thickEnd - green 0,255,0,
