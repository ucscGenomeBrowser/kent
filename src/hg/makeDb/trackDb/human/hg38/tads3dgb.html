Description

This track collection shows TAD domains from the 3D Genome Browser (3DGB) across 464 human Hi-C and Micro-C datasets on hg38. Each subtrack is one 3DGB dataset.

The 464 datasets include normal and cancer samples, baseline and perturbation samples, and samples from 28 organs and many cell types. They come from public Hi-C and Micro-C data, which 3DGB processed with its own pipeline. They can be filtered on the track configuration page (see below). The displayed domain intervals are 3DGB's own, with no UCSC re-calling, merging, lifting, or recurrence scoring.

Display Conventions and Configuration

Each subtrack is colored by organ. By default, 4 datasets are shown: GM12878, H1-ESC, IMR-90, and HMEC. The others can be turned on from the track configuration page. Mousing over a domain shows the dataset name, organ, and assay.

These 464 datasets are not a cross-comparable consensus. Each represents one dataset's own TAD calls, made by different laboratories on different samples. Coordinates are therefore not directly comparable across subtracks, and they are not directly comparable to the other TAD tracks in this set (which use different callers and resolutions). Because 3DGB calls TADs from Hi-C data analyzed in fixed-size 20 or 25 kb bins, domain edges are only accurate to roughly one bin. Domains do not cover the whole genome.

Subtracks can be filtered on the track configuration page by:

  • Organ: the organ of the sample (used for subtrack color).
  • Cell type: the cell type, where annotated by 3DGB ("(unspecified)" when 3DGB does not record one).
  • Assay: Hi-C or Micro-C.
  • Condition: whether the sample is normal or cancer.
  • Treatment: baseline (untreated) or perturbation (e.g. drug treatment, gene knockout, or other experimental manipulation).
  • Provenance: see below.
  • Year and Study: the publication year and the source data accession (e.g. a GEO series) that 3DGB re-processed.

Provenance facet

The Provenance facet has two values:

  • Only in this track (420 datasets): the source study is not used in any other UCSC track.
  • Also in another UCSC track (44 datasets):
    • Schmitt 2016 (11 datasets): shown in the Schmitt 2016 boundaries track
    • Rao 2014 (7 datasets): shown in the Rao 2014 Hi-C track on hg19, and among the studies whose maps are counted in the TAD boundary stability track
    • Dixon 2015 (5 datasets): among the studies whose maps are counted in the TAD boundary stability track
    • ENCODE (19 datasets): 18 of these datasets are from biosamples that are also in the ENCODE TADs track
    • 4DN Micro-C (2 datasets, H1-hESC and HFFc6): part of the Hi-C and Micro-C track

Domains for these datasets can still differ from the other tracks, because 3DGB processed each study with its own pipeline.

Methods

TADs were called by the 3D Genome Browser from the cooltools insulation score, at 20 or 25 kb resolution. The 3DGB TAD file for each dataset was converted to a four-column bigBed (chromosome, start, end, dataset name). Domain coordinates were not changed. The dataset metadata used for the Organ, Cell type, Assay, Year, and Study facets was copied from the 3D Genome Browser. The Condition and Treatment values were assigned by UCSC from the 3D Genome Browser's dataset descriptions, and the Provenance values were assigned by UCSC as described above.

Data Access

The raw data can be explored interactively with the Table Browser or the Data Integrator. For programmatic access, the track can be accessed using the Genome Browser's REST API. The underlying bigBed files can be downloaded from our download server. The complete original datasets are available from the 3D Genome Browser.
