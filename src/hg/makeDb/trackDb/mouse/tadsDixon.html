Description

These tracks show topologically associating domains (TADs), the original "topological domains" defined by Dixon et al. 2012, for two mouse cell types:

  • mouse embryonic stem cells (mESC)
  • mouse cortex

The calls were made on mm9 and are displayed here lifted to this assembly (see Methods).

Display Conventions and Configuration

The two cell types are separate subtracks. Domain edges are only accurate to about ±20 kb (see Methods). Domains do not cover the whole genome.

Methods

TAD domains were called by Dixon et al. 2012 with the directionality-index hidden Markov model at 40 kb resolution (the published "Combined" replicate call set: 2,200 mESC and 1,518 cortex domains, mm9). UCSC lifted the mm9 coordinates to this assembly with liftOver (a small percentage of domains that did not map cleanly were dropped) and reformatted them to bigBed. No re-calling was performed. Lifted domains that came out more than twice their original size were also removed.

Data Access

The raw data can be explored interactively with the Table Browser or the Data Integrator. For programmatic access, the track can be accessed using the Genome Browser's REST API. The underlying bigBed files can be downloaded from our download server.
