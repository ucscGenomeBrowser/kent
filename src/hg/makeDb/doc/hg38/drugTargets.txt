# Notes on the input:
# - 2145 of the 2660 assessed_variants rows are blank spreadsheet lines.
# - Variants are placed by their genomic HGVS. 7 variants have only a transcript HGVS
#   and are mapped with hgvsToVcf (DYSF exon 4 deletion x2, AP4M1 x2, the CAG repeat
#   expansions in ATN1 and ATXN7 x2, placed on the first base of the repeat).
# - The registry's separate Chromosome / Genomic Position / Reference Allele columns
#   disagree with the HGVS for 22 variants and 2 projects (hg19 positions, alleles on
#   the transcript strand, typos) and are not used for variants. The HGVS agrees with
#   the transcript HGVS everywhere.
# - Two genomic variants are in SPDI notation (NC_:pos:del:ins, 0-based position).
# - Projects without a variant: 3 are placed on the genomic range given in the
#   Genomic Position column (ANKRD17, AP4M1, LNPK), 2 knockdown projects on the MANE
#   Select transcript of their gene (C9orf72, GRIN1).
# - Marketed drugs are placed on the MANE Select transcript of their gene, not on the
#   registry coordinates (wrong for ADA, 1.4 Mbp, and for DDC, which had the DCC
#   position). Upstaza is listed under DCC, which is corrected to DDC in the script
#   while the registry still has DCC.
# - Category spellings (eligibility, approach, pathomechanism etc.) are normalized with
#   tables in n1cRegistryToBed.py. An unknown spelling stops the script.
