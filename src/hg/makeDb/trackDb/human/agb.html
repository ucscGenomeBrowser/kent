Description

This track is part of the SNV Frequencies collection. It shows rare coding variants from the Autism Gene Browser (AGB), which presents the exome sequencing results of the Autism Sequencing Consortium study by Satterstrom, Auwerx, Fu et al. (2026). The study combined exomes of more than 62,000 autistic individuals and 33,000 individuals without recorded autism from family-based and case-control cohorts. The track contains 7 million rare variants (maximum allele frequency 0.1%) that are protein-truncating, missense or synonymous.

The data contains allele counts only and no allele numbers, so no allele frequencies can be calculated per variant. The counts are split by study design: de novo variants in autistic probands and in their non-autistic siblings, inherited variants that the parents transmitted or did not transmit to the autistic proband, and variants in autism cases and controls from the case-control cohorts.

Display Conventions

Each variant is drawn as a VCF item. Clicking a variant shows its details page with the counts from all study designs, the consequence on the canonical transcript, the HGVS notation, the MPC and AlphaMissense scores, the gnomAD allele frequency and the variant class used by the study:

  • PTV: protein-truncating variant (stop gained, frameshift, splice donor or acceptor, or a variant predicted to affect splicing elsewhere)
  • Mis2: missense variant with MPC ≥ 2 and AlphaMissense ≥ 0.97
  • Mis1: missense variant meeting one of these two thresholds
  • Mis0: missense variant meeting neither threshold
  • synonymous

We added four summary fields: AC_AUT, the allele count in autistic individuals (de novo and transmitted in probands, plus cases), AC_NONAUT, the allele count in non-autistic individuals (de novo in siblings, plus controls), AC, the sum of these two, and AC_UNTRANS, the count of parental alleles not transmitted to the proband. The track can be filtered on the autistic and non-autistic counts and on the de novo count in probands.

Methods

The study aggregated exome sequencing data from four family-based cohorts, the Autism Sequencing Consortium (ASC), the Simons Simplex Collection (SSC), SPARK and the GeneDx clinical testing laboratory, plus published de novo variants from autistic individuals in the DDD study, and from three case-control cohorts: iPSYCH, PAGES, and SPARK cases matched to UK Biobank controls. In total, it includes 38,680 autistic probands, 9,567 unaffected siblings, 23,749 cases and 23,749 controls. Data were aligned to GRCh38 and variants called with GATK HaplotypeCaller or taken from joint callsets, depending on the cohort, then harmonized with Hail. De novo variants were called with a custom caller and filtered on allele balance, depth and strand bias. Rare variants were defined as having a maximum allele frequency of 0.1% in gnomAD and within the dataset, with lower, dataset-specific thresholds for the case-control data. Only protein-truncating, missense and synonymous variants were analyzed. See the reference below for details.

The variant results VCF file AGB_variant_results.vcf.bgz (release of October 12, 2026) was downloaded from the AGB downloads page. It is already on GRCh38 and needed no liftover. A script added descriptions to the INFO fields, which were empty in the original file, replaced spaces in the consequence names with underscores, and added the summary counts described above. The source file has 20 variants that occur twice with the same position and alleles, one copy classified as PTV with the de novo count and the other as Mis0 with the inherited counts. These were merged into one record each, summing the counts and listing both classes, for example PTV|Mis0. This leaves 7,045,130 of the 7,045,150 records. The steps are documented in our makeDoc file, and the script agbToVcf.py is in the scripts directory.

Data Access

Since the data can be downloaded from the AGB website, and does not seem to be under a license, we assume that we are allowed to redistribute it in VCF format. The data can be explored interactively with the Table Browser or the Data Integrator and exported from there to spreadsheet or tab-sep tables. From scripts, the data can be accessed through our API, track=agb.

For automated download and analysis, the VCF file agb.vcf.gz and its tabix index can be downloaded from our download server. The original data, together with gene-level results, can be downloaded from the AGB downloads page.
