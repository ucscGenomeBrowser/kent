About My Annotations

My Annotations is your own track of variants and other features. Annotations are saved with your Genome Browser account, so only you can see them unless you share them, and they are still there the next time you log in.

  • The Position box takes a position, a gene symbol or an HGVS term. A gene or an HGVS term is looked up and fills in the rest of the form for you; check it over before you save.
  • Annotate short variants, copy number variants, transcripts, or anything else you want to mark.
  • Click an annotation in the track to see its details, change it, or remove it.
  • Keep your annotations in more than one track, so you can show one and hide another. The Track box says which one this annotation goes into, and the + next to it makes a new one.
  • Share a link to a track with other people, either read only or with permission to edit.

My Annotations is a new feature, and we would like to hear what you think of it. Please send your feedback to genome-www@soe.ucsc.edu.

Annotations are stored on shared UCSC infrastructure. Do not enter patient data, identifiable clinical records, or any other protected health information.
