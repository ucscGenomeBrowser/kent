Description

These tracks show topologically associating domains (TADs), the original "topological domains" defined by Dixon et al. 2012, for two human cell types:

  • H1 human embryonic stem cells (hESC)
  • IMR90 fetal lung fibroblasts

Display Conventions and Configuration

Domain edges are only accurate to about ±20 kb (see Methods). Domains do not cover the whole genome. Dixon et al. define the gaps between domains as either "topological boundary regions" or "unorganized chromatin", depending on their size. The authors did not publish a confidence score for each domain. These domains are not directly comparable to the other TAD tracks.

Methods

Domains were identified with a directionality-index hidden Markov model on 40 kb Hi-C contact matrices (Dixon et al., 2012). The published combined-replicate domain calls for hESC and IMR90 (Supplementary Table S3) are on hg18. They were lifted to this assembly with the UCSC liftOver tool, and a small fraction of domains that did not map cleanly were dropped. Lifted domains that came out more than twice their original size were also removed.

Data Access

The raw data can be explored interactively with the Table Browser or the Data Integrator. For programmatic access, the track can be accessed using the Genome Browser's REST API. The underlying bigBed files can be downloaded from our download server.
