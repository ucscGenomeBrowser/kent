Description

This track is part of the Fusion Genes collection. It shows the same fusion events as the FusionGDB track, but instead of the breakpoint, each partner gene is drawn as the part of its transcript that is kept in the fusion transcript: for the 5' partner from the start of the transcript to the breakpoint, for the 3' partner from the breakpoint to the end of the transcript. See the FusionGDB page for the data sources, the colors and the reading frame annotation.

Display Conventions and Configuration

Features are colored by reading frame, as in the FusionGDB track. The thick part is the coding sequence that is kept. The codon frames shown when codon coloring is switched on are those of the original transcript, which is the frame of the fusion protein only for in-frame fusions.

FusionGDB gives the strand of the fusion sequence, not of the genes. For many ChiTaRS fusions, which come from GenBank ESTs and mRNAs, the GenBank sequence is the reverse complement of the transcript. In these cases both genes are on the other strand than given in FusionGDB, the 3' partner comes first in the transcript, and we draw the fusion with the roles of the two genes swapped. If only one of the two genes is on the other strand, the fusion is drawn as given by FusionGDB. Both cases are noted in the mouseover and on the details page.

Methods

For each breakpoint pair, FusionGDB lists all pairs of GENCODE V19 transcripts of the two genes with their reading frame annotation. We took the transcript pair with the best reading frame (in-frame, then frame-shift, then any other) and, among those, the transcripts tagged appris_principal, then CCDS, then basic, then the one with the longest coding sequence. For breakpoint pairs without frame annotation, we took the best GENCODE V19 transcript of the gene by the same rules, among the transcripts that contain the breakpoint. Each transcript was cut at the breakpoint, which is the last kept base of the 5' partner and the first kept base of the 3' partner. For hg38, the features were converted with liftOver.

In FusionGDB, 183 gene symbols from ChiTaRS had been converted to dates by a spreadsheet program, e.g. 9-Sep for SEPT9 or 1-Sep-04 for SEPT14. We changed them back to the GENCODE V19 symbol (SEPT, MARCH, MARC or DEC genes) whose transcript contains the breakpoint. Of the 105,783 partner features on hg19, 90,986 are drawn from a FusionGDB transcript pair and 8,334 from a GENCODE transcript of the gene. For 6,463, no GENCODE V19 transcript of the gene contains the breakpoint, and only the breakpoint is shown as a 1 bp feature. In 3,451 fusion events both genes are on the other strand than the fusion sequence and the roles were swapped, and in 8,059 only one gene is. On hg38, 84 features could not be converted with liftOver and are not shown. Note that FusionGDB's in-frame annotation is not always the same as a strict reading frame check: for about a quarter of the in-frame breakpoint pairs, the number of coding bases kept from the 5' partner does not match the codon frame at the breakpoint of the 3' partner.

The commands are in the makeDoc file and the scripts are in our source repository.

Data Access

Because of the FusionGDB terms of use, this track cannot be exported with the Table Browser. See the FusionGDB page for how to obtain the original data.
