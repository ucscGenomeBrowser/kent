Description

This track is part of the Fusion Genes collection. It shows fusion gene breakpoints from FusionGDB, a database of human fusion genes and their predicted effect on the encoded protein. The track contains about 53,000 distinct breakpoint pairs of 48,000 fusion genes.

The breakpoints come from three data sets:

  • TumorFusions: fusions called with PRADA in TCGA tumor RNA-seq samples (Hu et al. 2018)
  • PanCanAtlas: fusions called with STAR-Fusion, EricScript and BREAKFAST in TCGA tumor RNA-seq samples (Gao et al. 2018)
  • ChiTaRS: chimeric transcripts from GenBank ESTs and mRNAs (ChiTaRS 3.1, Gorohovski et al. 2017). These have no sample or cancer type.

Display Conventions and Configuration

Each fusion is shown as two 1 bp features named after the fusion, e.g. TMPRSS2::ERG: one at the breakpoint in the 5' partner gene and one at the breakpoint in the 3' partner gene. The strand is the strand of the partner gene at that breakpoint. The same fusion gene can have several breakpoint pairs, and every pair is a separate feature. Each feature has an ID made of the fusion name, the number of the breakpoint pair and the partner, e.g. TMPRSS2::ERG.3.5p, shown on the details page. Hover over a feature to see its partner gene, reading frame, number of TCGA samples and sources. The details page links to the breakpoint of the partner gene.

Features are colored by the effect of the fusion on the reading frame. FusionGDB annotated each breakpoint pair for all pairs of GENCODE V19 transcripts of the two genes. If at least one transcript pair is in-frame, the feature is shown as in-frame.

  In-frame: both breakpoints are in coding exons and the fusion transcript keeps the reading frame of the 3' gene
  Frame-shift: both breakpoints are in coding exons, but the fusion shifts the reading frame of the 3' gene
  Outside CDS: at least one breakpoint is in a UTR or intron in all transcript pairs, or FusionGDB has no reading frame annotation for this breakpoint pair

A breakpoint outside the coding sequence does not mean that the fusion makes no protein. In TMPRSS2::ERG, for example, the 5' UTR of TMPRSS2 is joined to ERG, and ERG protein is made from its own start codon under the control of the TMPRSS2 promoter.

The track can be filtered by reading frame, source, TCGA cancer type and number of TCGA samples. Fusion names such as TMPRSS2::ERG, TMPRSS2-ERG or TMPRSS2_ERG can be typed into the position box.

Methods

FusionGDB collected fusion breakpoints from ChiTaRS 3.1 (17,860 fusion genes), TumorFusions (15,854 fusion genes in 5,517 TCGA samples) and the TCGA PanCanAtlas fusion calls of Gao et al. (23,944 fusion genes in 6,071 samples), all on hg19. For every breakpoint pair, FusionGDB located both breakpoints in all GENCODE V19 transcripts of the two genes. A transcript pair was called in-frame if both breakpoints are in coding exons and the length of the fusion transcript from the start codon of the 5' gene to the 3' gene's stop codon is a multiple of three, and frame-shift if both are in coding exons but it is not. Other combinations were labeled by the breakpoint location, e.g. 5UTR-3CDS. See Kim and Zhou 2019 for details.

We downloaded the files TCGA_ChiTaRS_combined_fusion_information_on_hg19.txt and TCGA_ChiTaRS_combined_fusion_ORF_analyzed_gencode_h19v19.txt from the FusionGDB download page. The first file has 65,780 rows, one per fusion, sample and source. We merged rows with identical breakpoints in both genes into 52,994 breakpoint pairs and wrote one feature for each partner gene. The breakpoint positions in the file are 1-based. For the TCGA data, about 95% of them are the last base of an exon of the 5' gene or the first base of an exon of the 3' gene in GENCODE V19. In 205 breakpoint pairs from ChiTaRS, the chromosome of one partner gene is unknown in FusionGDB, and only the other partner is shown. For hg38, we converted the breakpoints with liftOver. Mitochondrial breakpoints are on the rCRS sequence, called chrMT on hg19 and chrM on hg38, and were not converted. 14 breakpoints could not be converted to hg38 and are not shown there, while their partner is. The commands are in the makeDoc file and the scripts are in our source repository.

Data Access

The FusionGDB data is free for academic research. Its terms of use do not allow redistribution, so this track cannot be exported with the Table Browser. The original data can be downloaded from the FusionGDB download page. Commercial use requires a license from FusionGDB.

The download page has tab-separated text files, all with hg19 coordinates. The main files are:

  • TCGA_ChiTaRS_combined_fusion_information_on_hg19.txt: one row per fusion, sample and source, with the gene, chromosome, breakpoint and strand of both partners. This track was built from this file.
  • TCGA_ChiTaRS_combined_fusion_ORF_analyzed_gencode_h19v19.txt: the reading frame annotation for every pair of GENCODE V19 transcripts of the two genes.
  • Three files for the in-frame transcript pairs, with the sequence of the fusion protein (..._In-frame_100k_check_aminoacid_seq_new.txt), of the fusion coding sequence (..._In-frame_100k_check_cds_seq.txt) and of the fusion transcript (..._In-frame_100k_check_transcript_seq.txt).
  • Further files with protein features retained in the fusion, protein-protein interactions, related drugs and disease associations of the fusion genes.
