Description

Non-coding RNAs are transcripts that function as RNA instead of being translated into protein. This collection groups tracks of non-coding RNA genes and transcripts:

  • MirGeneDB 3.0: microRNA genes curated by MirGeneDB, with their arms and predicted secondary structures
  • miRBase 22.1: microRNA precursors registered in miRBase, with their mature miRNAs and predicted secondary structures
  • sno/miRNA: C/D and H/ACA box snoRNAs, scaRNAs and microRNAs from snoRNABase and miRBase 22
  • tRNA Genes: transfer RNA genes predicted with tRNAscan-SE
  • lincRNA TUCP and lincRNA RNA-Seq: lincRNA and TUCP transcripts and their expression across tissues, lifted from hg19

Each track has its own description page with its methods, data access and references.
