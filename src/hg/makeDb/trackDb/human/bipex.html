Description

This track is part of the SNV Frequencies collection. It shows variants from the Bipolar Exome (BipEx) browser, which presents the BipEx 2.0 study by Liao et al. (2026). The study combined exome sequencing data of more than 64,000 individuals with bipolar disorder and 168,000 controls from 88 cohorts in 22 countries and five continental ancestry groups. The track contains 32 million variants, most of them rare, in and around protein-coding genes.

For every variant, the track shows the allele counts and allele numbers in bipolar disorder cases and in controls, so allele frequencies can be compared between the two.

Display Conventions

Each variant is drawn as a VCF item. Clicking a variant shows its details page with the consequence on the canonical transcript, the HGVS notation, and the missense scores used by the study: MPC, AlphaMissense, MisFit-S, popEVE and the percentile of their combined rank (misrank_percentile). The study called missense variants above the 96th percentile of this combined rank "damaging" (missense_passing). The field in_analysis marks the variants that were used in the gene burden tests.

We added these fields to the original counts (ac_case, an_case, ac_ctrl, an_ctrl):

  • AF_CASE: allele frequency in bipolar disorder cases, with AC_CASE and AN_CASE
  • AF_CTRL: allele frequency in controls, with AC_CTRL and AN_CTRL
  • AC, AN, AF: cases and controls together

The track can be filtered on these three frequencies.

Methods

BipEx 2.0 combined whole-exome (WES), whole-genome (WGS) and blended genome-exome (BGE) sequencing data, including the samples of BipEx 1.0. WES and WGS data were jointly called with gnomAD v4.0 using GATK HaplotypeCaller, and WGS data were limited to exome target intervals. BGE data were generated with Twist Alliance Clinical Research Exome capture on Illumina NovaSeq and called with GATK or DRAGEN. Genotypes were required to have DP ≥ 10, GQ ≥ 20 and, for heterozygotes, an allele balance ≥ 0.3; variants failing VQSR (or the DRAGEN equivalent), outside exome targets, in low-complexity regions or with more than six alternate alleles were removed. After sample QC by callset, ancestry and relatedness, 232,536 individuals (64,435 cases, 168,101 controls) and 48,720,030 variants remained. Variants were annotated with VEP v95 on the Ensembl canonical transcript. Gene tests used variants with a minor allele frequency of at most 0.01%, stratified by sequencing platform and ancestry. See the reference below for details.

The variant results VCF file BipEx_v2_variant_results.vcf.bgz (release of August 7, 2026) was downloaded from the BipEx downloads page. It is already on GRCh38 and needed no liftover. A script added descriptions to the INFO fields, which were empty in the original file, and added the summary fields described above. The source file has 48,720,030 records. 16,591,560 of them have an annotation but no case and control counts; these were removed, which leaves 32,128,470 variants. The steps are documented in our makeDoc file, and the script erbToVcf.py is in the scripts directory.

Data Access

Since the data can be downloaded from the BipEx website, and does not seem to be under a license, we assume that we are allowed to redistribute it in VCF format. The data can be explored interactively with the Table Browser or the Data Integrator and exported from there to spreadsheet or tab-sep tables. From scripts, the data can be accessed through our API, track=bipex.

For automated download and analysis, the VCF file bipex.vcf.gz and its tabix index can be downloaded from our download server. The original data, together with gene-level results, can be downloaded from the BipEx downloads page.
