Genome Browser Logos and Branding

This page collects the Genome Browser logos, colors, fonts, and templates in one place. Use these files for posters, slides, talks, websites, and collaborations. The visual identity was created in 2026 with UC Santa Cruz Baskin Engineering Communications to mark the Genome Browser's 25th anniversary.

Logos

There are three versions of the logo. Each comes in four color options. Pick the one with the most contrast against your background:

  • Full color for white or light backgrounds.
  • Full color, reversed for dark backgrounds.
  • Single color (primary blue) for light backgrounds.
  • Single color, reversed (white) for dark or photo backgrounds.

Use SVG for websites and documents, PNG where SVG is not supported, and the PDF print pack for anything going to a printer. The print pack has RGB, CMYK, and Pantone (PMS) versions.

Colors

The primary colors are primary blue, medium blue, and yellow, in line with UC Santa Cruz branding. Accent colors go with the primary and neutral colors but should be used sparingly, never as the main color of a piece. Leave plenty of white space.

Typography

Roboto is the UC Santa Cruz typeface for print and digital materials. It is free from Google Fonts. Mix weights (Thin, Light, Regular, Medium, Bold, Black) to set up a hierarchy, but keep to two or three styles in one piece so it stays easy to read.

Supporting graphics

The bar pattern gives materials a shared look. Use it to frame content, add texture, or balance a layout, but not to compete with the content next to it. It can be single color or use the full palette, with the primary colors leading. See the supporting graphics guide (PDF).

To accelerate scientific discovery and human health advancements by making genomic knowledge freely accessible, interconnected, and useful to researchers worldwide.
