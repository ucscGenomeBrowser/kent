Description

This track collection shows TAD domains from ENCODE for 117 human biosamples on hg38 (ENCODE calls them "contact domains"). Each subtrack is one biosample.

ENCODE provides domains for many human biosamples across hundreds of files. This track shows one representative experiment per biosample. By default, 14 biosamples are shown: GM12878, K562, HepG2, HCT116, IMR-90, A549, HL-60/S4, heart left and right ventricle, dorsolateral prefrontal cortex, ovary, pancreas, transverse colon, and motor neuron. The others can be turned on from the track configuration page.

Display Conventions and Configuration

Subtracks are colored by organ. Mousing over a domain shows the biosample and the Arrowhead corner score. A higher corner score means the spot is more likely to be a domain corner. The details page also reports Arrowhead's upper/lower variance and sign scores, rounded to three decimal places. The Arrowhead calls use a different algorithm and a finer resolution (5 or 10 kb) than the other TAD tracks, produce smaller domains, and are not directly comparable to them.

Subtracks can be filtered on the track configuration page by:

  • Organ
  • Biosample type (cell line, tissue, primary cell, in vitro differentiated)
  • Assay (intact or in situ Hi-C)
  • Life stage
  • Calls: "Arrowhead (hg38)" for 112 biosamples, or "Lifted from hg19" for 5 (LNCaP clone FGC, NCI-H460, RPMI7951, SJCRH30, SK-N-MC). These 5 were produced by the Dekker lab on hg19 and lifted to hg38. Their domains do not overlap each other, are much larger (median about 760 kb), and have no Arrowhead scores.

In the downloadable files and Table Browser output, scores that are not shown on the details page are written as 0. These are missing scores, not real scores of 0.

Methods

For 111 biosamples, the domains were produced by the ENCODE uniform Hi-C processing pipeline, which is based on Juicer and calls domains with Arrowhead on hg38. A549's domains come from a separate Hi-C pipeline submitted to ENCODE by the Reddy lab. For the other 5, ENCODE provides domains produced by the Dekker lab on hg19 and lifted to hg38.

For each biosample, one experiment was selected, using these preferences in order:

  • files with the most complete set of Arrowhead scores
  • an experiment that ENCODE does not mark as perturbed (treated or genetically modified)
  • files that ENCODE marks as its preferred default
  • intact Hi-C, then in situ Hi-C, then dilution Hi-C
  • the largest total size of domain files

The domains from all of the selected experiment's files were then combined:

  • Each record that lists the same domain as a pair of anchors was reduced to a single domain interval. Records whose anchors differ (loops) were not used.
  • The Arrowhead scores were kept where ENCODE provides them, rounded to three decimal places.
  • When two domains had both endpoints within 5 kb of each other, only the one with the higher corner score was kept.

The chosen ENCODE experiment accession is given in each subtrack's long label.

Data Access

The raw data can be explored interactively with the Table Browser or the Data Integrator. For programmatic access, the track can be accessed using the Genome Browser's REST API. The underlying bigBed files can be downloaded from our download server. The original data files are also available from the ENCODE portal.
