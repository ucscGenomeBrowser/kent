Description

Many drugs act on the product of a single gene, and a growing number act on the gene's RNA directly. Antisense oligonucleotides (ASOs), for example, are short synthetic nucleic acids that bind a transcript and change its splicing or cause its degradation. Some can be designed for the variant of a single patient.

This collection shows genes and variants that are targets of genetic therapies, either already on the market, in development, or assessed for whether a therapy could be developed. It contains these tracks:

  • N1C Assessed Variants: about 500 disease variants that the N=1 Collaborative and other groups assessed for eligibility for a variant-specific ASO therapy
  • N1C N-of-1 Projects: 91 projects that develop a therapy for a single patient or a few patients
  • N1C Marketed Therapies: approved genetic therapies, shown on the gene they target

The three tracks come from the N1C Gene Registry of the N=1 Collaborative.
